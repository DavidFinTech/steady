For gig workers, freelancers, and independent contractors in NYC. Start with the situation closest to yours; each button takes you directly to the organization that can help.

FREELANCER-SPECIFIC

“I want to explore coverage for independent workers.”

Freelancers Union brings together health insurance information and partner offerings for self-employed workers.

Compare plan availability, provider networks, deductibles, and what each product actually covers. Some offerings are care memberships rather than comprehensive insurance.

Explore freelancer health options ↗

Independent organization with insurance partners; it discloses commissions on plan purchases. Availability and terms vary.

FREE NEW YORK ENROLLMENT HELP

“My income changes. I need help applying.”

NY State of Health assistors offer free help understanding coverage options and completing an application.

Ask how to estimate self-employment income, report changes, and check possible financial assistance. Bring questions about your household and income records.

Find an enrollment assistor ↗

Official New York marketplace support. An assistor can explain enrollment timing and eligibility; Steady does not determine either.

NYC CARE ACCESS

“I’m uninsured and need an affordable care option.”

NYC Care is a healthcare access program through NYC Health + Hospitals for eligible New Yorkers who cannot afford or do not qualify for health insurance.

NYC Care is not health insurance. Contact the program for screening, enrollment help, and information on fees based on household size and income.

See NYC Care enrollment steps ↗

Call 646-692-2273 · Services are through NYC Health + Hospitals.

Before choosing a plan: check whether your doctor and prescriptions are covered, compare total costs beyond the monthly premium, and confirm enrollment dates with the provider. Being a gig worker alone does not guarantee eligibility or a discounted plan.

ILLUSTRATIVE PERSONA
A class deadline on Monday. A client pays late. Rent is due Friday. Finding benefits becomes another unpaid job.

As students, we connect this idea to balancing classes, side gigs, and living costs. Our hypothesis: a weekly cash plan can make it easier to prioritize healthcare, everyday wellness, retirement savings, and legal questions when there is no employer benefits team.

This story is a design assumption. Interviews have not been conducted; the research plan below is part of the proposed next step.

Evidence and open questions

Grounded in existing support.
Ready to be tested with people.

What already exists

Official health marketplaces guide coverage exploration; NYC provides freelance rights and complaint resources. Steady proposes a shared starting point connecting these services with weekly planning.

What we still need to learn

Interview five NYC freelancers, including students with side gigs, about late payments, healthcare, gym access, retirement saving, and keeping tax records. Ask them to show their current process.

How we would evaluate it

Observe whether people can build a plan, find an appropriate official resource, and explain their next step. Target: four of five pilot participants complete these tasks without help. This is a proposed target.

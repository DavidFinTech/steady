A few things worth knowing.

Does Steady determine whether I qualify? ＋

No. Each program or agency reviews your circumstances and determines eligibility. Follow the official links for current rules and application steps.

Does the calculator save my financial information? ＋

No. Calculations happen on this page. Inputs are not sent to a server or saved by Steady. Reloading restores the sample values.

What if my income changes month to month? ＋

Use a realistic annual projection and revisit it as work changes. Keep income and expense records, and ask your Marketplace or tax professional how to update estimates.
